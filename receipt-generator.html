How it works

What to include on a receipt

Your business name and tax ID, the client's name, a unique receipt number, the issue and due dates, each item with quantity and rate, any tax or discount, the total due and how to pay you. US receipts usually also show your business name and address, payment terms and, where required, your sales tax ID.

How the total is worked out

The subtotal is quantity × rate for every line. The discount percentage comes off the subtotal first, and tax is then applied to the discounted amount. Total due = (subtotal − discount) × (1 + tax %).

Tips for clean record keeping

State the due date and payment method clearly, send the receipt the day the work is done, and add late-payment terms in the notes. Use one receipt number sequence so every receipt can be traced.

Frequently asked questions

Is this receipt generator free?

Yes. You can create, copy and print as many receipts as you want, with no account or watermark.

How do I save the receipt as a PDF?

Click Print / Save as PDF, then choose Save as PDF as the destination in your browser's print window.

Can I add sales tax to my receipt?

Yes. Enter your tax rate and it is applied to the amount after discount. Put your EIN or state sales tax ID in the Tax ID field.

Is my receipt data saved anywhere?

No. Everything stays in your browser and is cleared when you close or refresh the page, so copy or print the receipt before you leave.

What should my receipt number look like?

Use a unique, rising sequence such as RCT-0001, RCT-0002. Do not reuse numbers, because accountants and tax authorities expect each receipt to be identifiable.
