Sales Tax Calculator: Add or Remove Sales Tax

Add tax to a price, or take it out of a tax-inclusive price. Works with any state, county or city rate.

How it works

The two formulas

To add tax: total = price × (1 + rate ÷ 100). To remove tax from an inclusive price: price before tax = total ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100). The tax amount is the difference between the two.

Worked example

At a 7% rate, a price of $100.00 becomes $107.00 with tax, which is $7.00 of tax. Going the other way, $107.00 including tax is $100.00 before tax. US sales tax rates vary by state, county and city, so look up the rate where you sell.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate sales tax on a price?

Multiply the price by the tax rate divided by 100 to get the tax, then add it to the price. Or multiply the price by (1 + rate/100) for the total.

How do I remove sales tax from an inclusive price?

Divide the tax-inclusive price by (1 + rate/100). For example, $107 ÷ 1.07 = $100, so the sales tax is $7.

Does this work for VAT, sales tax and sales tax?

Yes. The math is the same. Enter the rate that applies where you are.

Which rate should I use?

Use the rate that applies to your goods or service under your local rules. Rates and categories change, so check with an accountant or the official tax website.
