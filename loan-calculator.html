How it works

Payment formula

Payment = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ ÷ ((1+r)ⁿ − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate (annual % ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of months. Total interest is payment × n − P.

Worked example

A $300,000 loan at 6.5% a year for 30 years has a monthly payment of about $1,896 (principal and interest only). Over 360 months you would pay about $682,633 in total, of which about $382,633 is interest. Taxes, insurance and fees are not included.

Frequently asked questions

How is a loan payment calculated?

Payment = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly payments.

Does a longer term lower my monthly payment?

Yes, but you pay more total interest. A shorter term raises the monthly payment and cuts the interest you pay.

Does this include processing fees or insurance?

No. It covers principal and interest only. Ask your lender about fees, insurance and prepayment charges.

Can I use it for home, car and personal loans?

Yes. It works for any loan repaid in fixed monthly payments on a reducing balance.
